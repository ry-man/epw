How to play

      

        
Redirect fire (Fire Folk) or water (Ice Inhabitants) through pipes to destroy the enemy's citizens. Most citizens destroyed wins.

        
Your turn

        
    
          
  • Tap a tile in your hand. Pipes: tap a board space to preview, Rotate as needed, then tap the same space again to confirm. Green dots show legal spaces.
    • 
          
  • A pipe must touch an existing pipe or factory; touching sides must both be open or both closed. A pipe may never carry both fire and water.
    • 
          
  • Bomb destroys any pipe. Construction replaces a pipe permanently (not one fed directly by a factory).
    • 
          
  • Move 1/2/3: tap your citizen, then a clear destination within that many steps.
    • 
          
  • Doubleplay: tap it twice. You must then play all your other tiles (plus extras drawn).
    • 
          
  • A tile with no legal move can be Discarded (it's replaced, no turn used).
    • 
          
  • Tap End turn when done.
    • 
        

        
Event cycle

        
After both players move, the counter advances. Space 4: both place a citizen. 6/8: Fire places. 7/9: Ice places. 10: factories produce — every citizen next to the open end of an enemy pipe is destroyed, along with any same-team citizens touching them (chain).

        
Citizens go within 2 steps of your factories or star (spawn) pipe tiles. For the first 2 cycles everything stays inside the centre 8×8 grid; then the board opens and each side places a new factory, repeating every 2 cycles.

        
Tip: use the 🔍 button to zoom the board and drag to pan.
